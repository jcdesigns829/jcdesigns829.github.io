About the work

  1. Supes

    A magazine cover that renders the hero in flat, posterized color so he pops against the photographed mountains behind him. A bold red serif masthead and a barcode make it read as a real newsstand cover.

  2. Spring Joy Chardonnay

    A wine label that turns a dandelion seed head into a lollipop, a playful take on spring. Bouncy yellow script, a green hillside and painted evergreens keep it light and nod to the Pine Valley name.

  3. Sour Strips

    Candy packaging built on a rainbow, with a speckled texture on every band that reads as the sugar coating on sour candy. Bubbly lettering follows the arcs, and the net weight and calories sit where a real package puts them.

  4. Mister Fahrenheit

    An album cover and track list of Queen songs, titled after a line in "Don't Stop Me Now." The rocket at liftoff and the thermometer bursting past 200 degrees come from the same song, and fiery lettering carries the heat across both panels.

  5. Radial Design

    A symmetrical study in black, gold and gray with an Art Deco feel: sunburst lines and mirrored imagery at the center, interlocking rings around the edge. With no type at all, composition and repetition do the work.

  6. The Flash

    A magazine spread that takes its palette straight from the costume, with yellow type on a solid red page facing a photo of the hero. A wide, squared typeface gives the headline and copy a sense of speed.
