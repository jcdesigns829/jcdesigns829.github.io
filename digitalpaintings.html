About the work

  1. The New Yorker

    A canyon road in one point perspective, painted with soft, airbrushed color inside crisp black outlines, so it reads as painting and drawing at once. Open Road, on the Traditional Painting page, is the same road painted by hand.

  2. Self Portrait 1

    A frontal self portrait with the face kept calm and simple, set against a storm of purple and yellow strokes. The two colors sit opposite each other on the color wheel, so the background vibrates while the face holds still.

  3. Self Portrait 2

    A bearded self portrait on the same structure as the first, with a wider palette. Loops of yellow, green, red, purple and blue frame the head like a halo, and the face carries more shading.

  4. Apple River

    A surreal digital collage that mixes a photographed branch, shaded green apples, flat triangular hills and a river filled with texture. Each element is rendered a different way, which is what makes the scene feel like a dream.
