The person behind the pixels & paint

Hi, I’m Jack.

Artist, designer, collector of references, and a believer that good work starts with a little curiosity.

A CREATIVE PRACTICE IN PROGRESS

Looking closely.
Making freely.

I’m a digital media graphic designer from Larchmont, New York, shifting into UI/UX and product design in the creative and brand design industry. I graduated from Marist University in 2025 with a B.S. in Digital Media and a concentration in Graphic Design.

I’ve always been interested in the work of artists who came before me. Looking at how they made things—their techniques, choices, and ways of seeing—gives me new ideas to explore in my own projects.

My work moves between digital painting, illustration, animation, graphic design, and traditional painting. I enjoy the process of trying a new medium, figuring out how it works, and letting a bit of play into the final result.
