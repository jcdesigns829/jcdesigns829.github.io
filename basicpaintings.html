About the work

  1. Living Room

    An interior built almost entirely from the primaries: a yellow wall, red curtains and a blue couch. Pale patches on the cushions echo the bright window panes above, tying the top and bottom of the painting together.

  2. Open Road

    A canyon road in one point perspective, with the yellow center line pulling the eye to the horizon and dry, broken strokes giving the asphalt its grit. The New Yorker, on the Digital Painting page, is the same road painted digitally.

  3. Spool

    A single spool enlarged until it fills the frame. Its warm orange body stands out against a dark background broken up by patches of pink, teal and lavender, and a flat cream tabletop keeps it grounded.

  4. Abstract

    A gestural abstraction built from blocks of red, orange, yellow and blue, cut through by dark, sweeping marks. Drips and rough edges are left in, keeping the energy of each stroke.

  5. Draped Form

    A study of draped fabric, built from light and dark planes of dusky purple on a cool blue ground. Two small blocks of yellow, purple's complement, sit at opposite corners and keep the eye moving around the form.

  6. Glass Jar

    Glass is mostly whatever sits behind it, so the jar is painted almost entirely through its highlights, with the red wall and pink table showing straight through. Traces of the pencil underdrawing are still visible along its edges.
