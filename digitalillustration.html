About the work

  1. Spring Bunny

    A spring scene with a bunny in blue overalls, a basket of decorated eggs and a row of flowers. Clean outlines on the bunny and dense scribbles in the grass and treetop make him stand out from the busy setting.

  2. Three Bears

    A picture book take on Goldilocks and the Three Bears. The bears line up from biggest to smallest outside their cabin while a tiny Goldilocks waves from the woods, all tied together with bold brown outlines and flat color.

  3. Walk in the Park

    Pure black line on white, with no color or shading. A smiling figure strolls along with a balloon, and the open white space between two scribbled bands of grass gives the drawing a light, carefree feel.

  4. Character Lineup

    A character sheet for a goblin warrior, with a turnaround of front, side and back views, thumbnail sketches along the top and weapon designs below. It shows the design moving from rough idea to a colored final.

  5. Robot Battle

    A crowded battle scene: a giant stacked robot at the center, smaller robots on brick towers, a satellite overhead and lightning in between. Soft shadows make each piece sit like a layered cutout, and heavy black strokes keep the frame moving.

  6. Splatter Cake

    A grayscale piece that sets a neat, tiered cake topped with cherries against a storm of black ink splatters. White highlights make some of the drops look wet and glossy, and swirling gray textures fill the background.

  7. Party Mouse

    A gray mouse dressed as a clown, with a rainbow wig, a zigzag ruff and oversized red shoes. Thick, flat strokes on a plain white background keep all the attention on the costume and the character's personality.
