About the work

  1. Character Turnaround

    A cartoon character turning a full circle in place, from front to profile to back and around again. Keeping the head, body and limbs consistent from every angle is what makes it read as one solid figure.

  2. Box Animation

    A red book wobbles on a gray box, tips off the edge, then bounces and tumbles to a stop on the floor. The wobble builds anticipation, and the book turns in perspective as it falls, which gives it believable weight.

  3. Bouncing Ball

    The classic first exercise in animation. The ball squashes on impact, stretches as it speeds up and loses height with each bounce, and the planned arc is left visible in gray so the path and timing are easy to follow.
