What the colors mean

  1. Blue

    Blue is my favorite color, so it takes the largest band in the mark.

  2. Teal

    Teal is for Tourette. It is the color of Tourette syndrome awareness.

  3. Red

    Red is for Marist, where I earned my degree in Digital Media.

  4. Orange

    Orange represents where I went to high school.
